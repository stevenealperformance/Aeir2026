How to read the quad muscle oxygen cards

Quad is the working muscle in cycling. At matched submax power:

  • Lower O₂Hb / higher HHb = more O₂ being extracted by the muscle (the signature of a strong, well-trained working muscle).
  • Higher O₂Hb / lower HHb = delivery exceeds demand or the muscle is producing the same power with less O₂ extracted (improved economy at the muscle).

The slope chart (%/s) shows the rate of change in SmO₂ within each step — gray bars are Test 1, red bars are Test 2. A positive value means the muscle is re-oxygenating during the step; a negative value means it is desaturating. A working muscle that desaturates faster at high intensity is generally a sign of strong extraction capacity.
