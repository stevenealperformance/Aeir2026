FEV1 = 3.65 L. Higher TV/FEV1 = more breath capacity used per breath, which lets the athlete hit the same VE at a lower breath rate. At RR 30 a 90% TV/FEV1 yields VE ≈ 100 L/min; at RR 60 the same VE only needs 50% TV/FEV1 — but fast/shallow wastes more on dead space. Typical endurance target: 75–85%.

Iso-VE breath patterns (FEV1 = 3.65 L)

To hit the same VE, you trade depth (TV) for rate (RR). The same VE can come from a deep / slow pattern or a shallow / fast one — but shallow / fast wastes more on dead space ventilation. Higher TV/FEV1 capacity unlocks the deep / slow option.
